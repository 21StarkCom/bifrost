    Choices in this design

    1. Jobs and flows are the lever. The look is the last step.
    2. Each element answers the challenge questions. A name, a size and a place each need a reason.
    3. A flow is tested end to end, by known paths and by agent users.
    4. You decide as a consumer, by a test drive. Moran Shor reviews the winner as the expert.
    5. The jobs go to every arm. Only the method is the bible's.
    6. Homes: the tool in draupnir, the loop in bifrost, the knowledge in vault-docs, the shell in sleipnir.
    7. The kit rule stays, and the kit is frozen for the proof.
